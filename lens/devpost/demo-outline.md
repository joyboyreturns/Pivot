# PIVOT demo outline

Target length: about 90 seconds. Record the interactive preview, including the controls and the scene as you move around it.

1. **The problem (0–15 s).** “Before lifting a sofa, can we get it through the door? PIVOT puts that question at the doorway, at real scale.” Show the whole sofa and opening, then move closer to the restriction.
2. **Narrow opening (15–35 s).** Point out the 0.80 m opening and the sofa box's 0.85 m smallest dimension. Explain that the best tested fixed box pose has a 5 cm shortfall. Pinch the arrow and scrub the ghost; read the unverified maneuver label.
3. **Compare (35–65 s).** Pinch the doorway marker to select 0.95 m. Show the result: a 90° turned box pose has 5 cm total clearance in its tightest doorway axis. Step through the ghost and scrub backward.
4. **What the result means (65–90 s).** “PIVOT checks a box at a fixed orientation against the opening. The transition and full route are not collision checked; this is a visualization, not moving advice.” Note that the virtual dimensions are supplied in advance, and a real sofa may deform or fit by another method.

Use a clean preview reset before recording. Keep the result and the unverified label in view when demonstrating motion. Show the project working rather than relying on slides.
