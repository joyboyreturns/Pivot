# PIVOT demo outline

Under 3 minutes (about 2:40). Record the Lens Studio Interactive Preview. Start from a clean preview on scenario 1. No music (or music you own). Keep the label and the verdict in view while the ghost moves.

1. **The problem (0:00-0:15).** "Before lifting a sofa: can it get through the door, up the hall, into the lift? PIVOT answers that for any object and any place." Show scenario 1 on the move table: model of the flat door, the sofa, three gate tokens.
2. **Blocked, and the fix (0:15-0:45).** "Won't fit, 5 cm too tight." The ghost replays and the label on it says **Unverified maneuver**: an illustration of a move that does not fit. Read the two tags: widen to 85 cm, or 5 cm lower, legs off. **Pinch "Widen to 85 cm"**: the route re-plans and flips to fits.
3. **Checked path (0:45-1:10).** Next scenario: 95 cm door. The ghost trail shows the whole move; dimension lines and "2.5 cm" appear on each side of the gap. The label says **Verified step**: the turn and the room to make it were checked.
4. **The hall is the problem (1:10-1:25).** Next: same door, 1.2 m hall. "No room to turn: the turn needs 2.28 m." "A doorway-only check would have said yes."
5. **A whole route (1:25-1:50).** Wardrobe by lift: pinch the lift token for the cabin, the last token for the unmeasured hall (**Unverified maneuver**). Hallway corner: the L-shaped plan, the sofa laid on its back swinging round, 1 cm at the corner. Pinch **Life size** and walk round it with WASD; labels turn to face you.
6. **Beyond furniture (1:50-2:30).** Show the touring flight case, then the gallery crate blocked at the side entrance. Pinch **Try another route**: the same crate clears the loading route. Show the workshop bench and goods lift comparison. Pinch **Swap object** to keep the measured route with a different load. Then briefly show editable measurements. Original sandbox sequence: Pinch **Your move**. Next place: terraced house. Next object: piano, bed, wardrobe. Grab the sofa's end handle and pull it in until the verdict flips; pull a door jamb out; add a gate. "Every number is yours; PIVOT re-plans the whole route and tells you what would fix it."
7. **How it was built and the limits (2:30-2:55).** "A real planner, 107 tests, and 12 LEAF scenarios run in the preview. Built by a coding agent through the Lens Studio MCP. Feel on real glasses is unproven: no device." Flash `design-brief.md` and a LEAF run.

Pitch points to say out loud: problem (moving day, not placement), who (buyers, movers, shops), why glasses (a model at arm's length, life size at the gate, hands free), what makes it different (route not spot, verified versus unverified, try-the-fix).
